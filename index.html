Transparency

How it all fits together

01

Runs on your machine

OpenPublisher runs in your browser and as a desktop app, with no proprietary cloud lock-in. Your documents stay yours.

02

Open hosting & edge AI

The web app, templates, docs, and downloads are delivered through GitHub, while AI runs across 7 dedicated Cloudflare FLUX.1 image nodes and 7 shared routed nodes (Groq and Gemini) with automatic failover.

03

Offline resilience

Even during a hosting or AI service disruption, the desktop editions for Windows, macOS, Linux, and FreeBSD keep editing and exporting .pub and PDF layouts offline.
